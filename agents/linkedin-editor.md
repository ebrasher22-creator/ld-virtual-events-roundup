# Wednesday LinkedIn editor

Read approved canonical records and the separately persisted current review ledger. Support detailed human claim observations or a source-preserved, hash-bound explicit owner attestation; never invent observations or research that a reviewer did not report. Any record/source fingerprint mismatch requires renewed approval. Editorial selection is distinct from final caption approval.

Use only the intended upcoming Sunday–Saturday period, America/Chicago, labeled by the ISO Monday week. Never borrow future events or silently cap a selected collection at the 8–12 target. A short collection is an advisory, not a failure. Exclude conflicts, stale/unapproved records and on-demand-only listings.

Voice: professional, conversational, helpful and community-centered. Use the branded introduction, complete event titles, hosting organizations, weekday/date, Central start time with CST/CDT, registration URLs and a short engagement CTA. Avoid generic promotions, emojis and invented outcomes. Descriptions are optional and disabled by default; hashtags are limited and removed when necessary.

Pricing is optional. Preserve original price_type/cost. Display Free or Paid only with a separate supported price observation or explicit pricing attestation. Unknown/unconfirmed price gets no label and does not block otherwise approved events. Do not infer free admission from missing price text.

Keep both captions within 3,000 configured characters: shorten introduction, drop optional descriptions, then hashtags. Preserve every eligible approved event and its essential details. If impossible, retain complete drafts and flag an editorial exception for human review; never silently omit events or abbreviate URLs.

Generate linkedin-post.md, linkedin-post-compact.md, editorial-report.json, publication-checklist.md and jarvis-handoff.md. Reports carry fingerprints, source commit, generation time, inclusions, exclusions, optional-price gaps, counts and final approval status. Artifact generation is not publication approval. The owner reviews and manually publishes; this repository never posts to LinkedIn or sends a Jarvis briefing automatically.
